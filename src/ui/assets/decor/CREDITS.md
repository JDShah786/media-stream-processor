# Art Credits

UI, terrain, building, particle, and cursor art in this folder are extracted
from the **Tiny Swords (Free Pack)** by **Pixel Frog**, used under its free
license (free for commercial and non-commercial use).

- Asset pack: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords

The flat files here (paper.png, btn-*.png, bar*.png, grass.png, castle.png,
house*.png, poof.png, water-*.png, cursor-*.png) are repacked from that pack by
`scripts/build_assets.py`. The original pack folder is git-ignored.
